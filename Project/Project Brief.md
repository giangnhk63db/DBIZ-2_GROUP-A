## **Problem**

From the process researched, we identified the following pain points:

- The preliminary quote relies only on information submitted by the car owner, and staff have no quick, accurate way to verify it. Carpla confirmed over Zalo on 30/09/2026 that the preliminary quote is based on the actual information the customer provides. The preliminary quote has no verified vehicle record to rest on. Without a report that consolidates the vehicle’s information, both owners and Carpla staff must rely on claims and photos instead of a consistent, checkable source. *(Source: Zalo message with Carpla, 30/09/2026)*   
- Owners must manually enter all 8 mandatory fields of the quote form (brand, model, year of manufacture, mileage, region, color, phone number and expected price), even though most of this data already exists in the ecosystem and is not well used. Carpla staff then contact the owner via Zalo and ask for new photos of the car before giving a specific, market-based quote. This costs the owner a lot of data-entry effort and forces many rounds of contact. *(Source: [Carpla, n.d](https://carpla.vn/thu-mua-xe))*  
- Carpla staff and owners go back and forth many times before a quote is given. After the form is submitted, staff contact the owner via Zalo and ask for new photos of the car before giving a specific, market-based quote, so the owner has to be contacted repeatedly and resend information  *(Source: Zalo message with Carpla, 30/09/2026.)*   
- Owners have no single, reliable place to check their car’s market price. To find out what their vehicle is worth, owners must ask several different parties and compare the answers themselves, which takes effort and gives no clear basis for the figures.  
- Vehicle information is scattered, so owners cannot easily track their vehicles. Data about a car sits in different places, and owners have no feature that integrates it in one view to follow the vehicle’s status over time.

## **Goal of the project**

The project delivers a Verified Vehicle Report that gives car owners a clearer view of their vehicles and makes the preliminary quote process faster, more reliable and less demanding. It aims to:

- Provide a feature that integrates all information for car owners to track their vehicles, so owners can follow their vehicle’s data in one place instead of looking it up across separate sources.  
- Provide a clear market price for owners to reference, so they do not have to ask several different parties to look up the price of their car.  
- Let Carpla staff verify owner-submitted information quickly and accurately, so a preliminary quote no longer depends only on what the owner claims.  
- Reduce manual data entry for owners by reusing vehicle data that already exists in the ecosystem to pre-fill the quote form.  
- Cut the back-and-forth between staff and owners before a preliminary quote, so the owner is not contacted repeatedly or asked to resend information that is already available.

Measures agreed with the client are in [the CVCA](https://github.com/giangnhk63db/DBIZ-2_GROUP-A/blob/b97524e0b4e917c04f3b9b0d181db5fec1e721fa/Context/CVCA%20After.md) (stakeholder requirements) and, in money terms, in [W6 cash flow](https://github.com/giangnhk63db/DBIZ-2_GROUP-A/blob/b97524e0b4e917c04f3b9b0d181db5fec1e721fa/Function%20List/w6-cash-flow.md).
